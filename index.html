CalFind Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Information We Collect

CalFind collects email address, date of birth, weight, height, sex, goals, food logs, and exercise data to provide calorie tracking and weight management services. Photos are processed for food identification and are not stored permanently.

How We Use Your Information

We use your data to calculate nutrition, track progress, and manage your subscription. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. And user can always delete his or her data or account from his or her device.
